## Why survey the author separately

Not to play the man. The reason is practical: Sophontic's public material uses vocabulary
that does not map to ML terms, and the mapping is often findable in the author's other
writing. Knowing which framework a term comes from is the difference between dismissing it
and translating it.

The survey also sets expectations honestly. This is a body of work in **philosophy of
consciousness and speculative physics**, not machine learning, and it is published in venues
without ML peer review. That does not make the engineering claims false. It does mean the
engineering claims arrive without the usual filters, so they need our own.

## The load-bearing finding

**The chain runs: attractor state → teleodynamics → geometric reasoning → Sophontic.**

By his own account, tracking the Attractor State emergence in 2025 led to *Principia
Cybernetica* and to co-founding "teleodynamic physics, philosophy, and machine learning
architecture". The company's technical programme is downstream of a metaphysical programme
that is downstream of an empirical observation about LLM self-conversation.

This matters for reading the claims, in both directions:

- It explains why "geometric density" is never defined in ML terms — **the concept originates
  in a framework where it was never an ML term.** It is a translation problem, not
  necessarily an evasion.
- It is a reason for caution about the *interpretation* of results, not about the results.
  A researcher who arrived at small-model reasoning via a theory of consciousness may
  reasonably find confirmation in outcomes that have duller explanations — which is exactly
  what [[library/ideas/data-quality-over-scale]] predicts is going on.
- It is **not** a reason to dismiss the flip-rate methodology, which stands entirely on its
  own and matches established contrast-set practice.

## One factual check

PatternThrone states Anthropic documented convergence "in 90–100% of self-interactions".
Anthropic's Claude 4 system card does report ~90–100% for **open-ended** self-interaction,
and a much lower ~13% within 50 turns when models are given **assigned tasks**. Quoting only
the first figure is technically accurate and rhetorically selective. Detail in
[[library/ideas/attractor-states]].

## What this source does not establish

- Nothing empirical about model performance. None of these works reports an ML experiment
  with a baseline.
- No operational definition of any of the geometric quantities, in any of the works surveyed.
- No independent review — repository preprints, self-published site.
