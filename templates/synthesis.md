## The combination

The position that none of the ingredient sources states alone. This is the part that
carries the risk, so mark it clearly: **inherited** vs **new**.

A synthesis earns its file when it is both *non-obvious* and *consequential* — it should
change what you would do or expect next. "These two authors agree" is not a synthesis.
"A's mechanism explains why B's method fails in the regime B never tested" is.

## Why nobody has said this

Usually because the two literatures do not read each other, or the vocabularies do not
translate. Occasionally because it is wrong in a way that is obvious from inside either
field — check that possibility honestly before proceeding.
